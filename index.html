04 — Safety Features

Safety engineered into
every kilometre.

Tap any card to see how it works. Every system is independent, redundant and monitored by a human in our control room.

+

Verified drivers

Enhanced background checks, 40 hours of annual training and continuous licence monitoring.

Every driver passes DBS / FBI-level screening, a driving-record audit and a psychometric safety assessment before their first route.

Licences, medicals and certifications are re-checked automatically every 30 days.

+

Live cabin cameras

Four wide-angle HD cameras cover the aisle, door and exterior — encrypted end to end.

Footage is encrypted at rest, auto-purged after 30 days, and only accessible to authorised safeguarding officers.

AI flags behaviour events such as standing in a moving vehicle within 1 second.

+

Seatbelt & seat sensors

Every seat reports occupancy and belt status to the driver console and the parent app.

An unbuckled seat triggers a gentle chime and a dash indicator; unresolved after 20 seconds, the control room is alerted.

Seat manifests are matched against the roster before the doors close.

+

Dual GPS + LTE-M

Two independent positioning systems with satellite fallback for rural routes.

Our telematics unit fuses GNSS, inertial dead-reckoning and vehicle CAN-bus data for uninterrupted tracking.

If one link drops, the second takes over in under 400 milliseconds — silently.

+

Panic button & 24/7 control room

One press opens a live audio channel and dispatches the nearest response unit.

Median control-room answer time is 4.1 seconds, with police and school escalation on a single console.

The driver never has to explain the situation twice — the operator already has the live map.

+

Speed & route compliance

Geofenced speed limits, harsh-braking scoring and automatic substitution if a driver falls below standard.

Each van is scored daily on 14 safety metrics; anything below 96% triggers an operational review.

Route deviations over 150m raise an immediate alert to the control room.
